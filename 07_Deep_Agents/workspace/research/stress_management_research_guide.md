# Stress Management Research Guide
## Key Strategies
1. **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** - Effective for reducing anxiety and stress.
2. **Mindfulness and Meditation** - Proven to lower cortisol levels and improve mental well-being.
3. **Exercise** - Helps in reducing anxiety and stress levels. Engaging in regular physical activity releases endorphins and alleviates anxiety.
4. **Nutrition** - A balanced diet supports mental health, especially omega-3 fatty acids and antioxidants.
5. **Social Support** - Maintaining strong relationships can provide emotional and practical support during stressful times.

## Additional Considerations
- Understand personal triggers for stress and develop coping mechanisms.
- Regularly revisit and update stress management strategies based on individual experiences.
