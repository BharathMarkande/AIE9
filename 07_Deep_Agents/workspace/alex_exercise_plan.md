# Alex's 2-Week Exercise Program

## Overview
A comprehensive 30-minute morning exercise routine designed to improve energy levels, build consistency, and support better sleep while being mindful of mild anxiety.

## Weekly Schedule
- **Week 1 & 2:**
  - Monday: Workout A (Full Body Strength)
  - Wednesday: Workout B (Cardio and Mobility)  
  - Friday: Workout C (Core and Stability)

## Workout Details

### Warm-Up (5 minutes for all workouts)
1. **March in Place** - 2 minutes
2. **Arm Circles** - 1 minute (30 seconds forward, 30 seconds backward)
3. **Leg Swings** - 1 minute (30 seconds each leg)
4. **Dynamic Stretching** - 1 minute (side lunges)

### Workout A - Full Body Strength (Monday)
- **Bodyweight Squats**: 2 sets of 10-12 reps
- **Modified Push-Ups** (knees on ground): 2 sets of 8-10 reps
- **Standing Dumbbell Shoulder Press** (light weights): 2 sets of 10 reps
- **Plank**: Hold for 20-30 seconds
- **Cool Down** (5 minutes): Stretch focusing on major muscle groups

### Workout B - Cardio and Mobility (Wednesday)
- **Brisk Walking or Light Jogging**: 10 minutes
- **High Knees**: 2 sets of 30 seconds
- **Side Lunges**: 2 sets of 10 reps (each side)
- **Cat-Cow Stretches**: 2 sets of 30 seconds
- **Cool Down** (5 minutes): Gentle stretches focusing on legs and back

### Workout C - Core and Stability (Friday)
- **Bird-Dog Exercise**: 2 sets of 10 reps (each side)
- **Glute Bridges**: 2 sets of 12 reps
- **Seated Russian Twists** (no weights or light weight): 2 sets of 8-10 reps (each side)
- **Standing Calf Raises**: 2 sets of 10-12 reps
- **Cool Down** (5 minutes): Stretching focusing on core and hips

## Week 2 Progressions
- Increase reps in each exercise by 1-3
- Reduce rest time between sets
- Increase plank hold duration slightly
- Extend high knees duration as comfortable

## Consistency Tips
1. **Set Regular Time**: Stick to morning workout schedule
2. **Prepare Night Before**: Lay out workout clothes
3. **Track Progress**: Keep a workout journal
4. **Find Accountability**: Share goals with friends/family

## Energy-Boosting Tips
- Incorporate short stretches during workday
- Use deep breathing before workouts if feeling anxious
- Practice gentle yoga poses when stressed

## Equipment Needed
- Light dumbbells (optional - can use water bottles)
- Exercise mat (optional)
- Comfortable workout clothes
