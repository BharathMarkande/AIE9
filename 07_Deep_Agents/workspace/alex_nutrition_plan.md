# Alex's 2-Week Vegetarian Nutrition Plan

## Overview
A comprehensive vegetarian meal plan designed to boost energy levels, support better sleep, and complement your morning exercise routine while being mindful of mild anxiety.

## Key Nutrients Focus
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Quinoa, oats, whole grains for sustained energy
- **Magnesium**: Spinach, nuts, seeds for relaxation and sleep
- **Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Chia seeds, flaxseeds for mental well-being
- **B Vitamins**: Whole grains, legumes for energy metabolism
- **Vitamin D**: Fortified plant milk, mushrooms for mood enhancement

## Pre/Post-Workout Nutrition Guidelines
**Pre-Workout (30-60 minutes before):**
- Banana
- Whole-grain toast with nut butter
- Small handful of mixed nuts
- Dried fruit

**Post-Workout (within 30 minutes):**
- Protein smoothie with spinach and almond milk
- Cottage cheese with pineapple
- Greek yogurt with honey
- Smoothie with protein powder, banana, and spinach

## Week 1 Meal Plan

### Day 1 (Monday - Workout Day)
- **Breakfast**: Oatmeal with almond milk, banana, and chia seeds
- **Lunch**: Quinoa salad with spinach, cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, lemon-tahini dressing
- **Dinner**: Stir-fried vegetables and tofu over brown rice
- **Snacks**: Greek yogurt with berries
- **Pre-Workout**: Banana
- **Post-Workout**: Protein smoothie with spinach and almond milk

### Day 2 (Tuesday - Rest Day)
- **Breakfast**: Smoothie with spinach, banana, and nut butter
- **Lunch**: Lentil soup with whole-grain bread
- **Dinner**: Veggie tacos with black beans, avocado, and salsa
- **Snacks**: Almonds and apple

### Day 3 (Wednesday - Workout Day)
- **Breakfast**: Overnight oats with berries and almond butter
- **Lunch**: Chickpea salad sandwich on whole-grain bread
- **Dinner**: Vegetable curry with quinoa
- **Snacks**: Hummus with carrot sticks
- **Pre-Workout**: Whole-grain toast with nut butter
- **Post-Workout**: Cottage cheese with pineapple

### Day 4 (Thursday - Rest Day)
- **Breakfast**: Chia pudding with almond milk and kiwi
- **Lunch**: Buddha bowl with quinoa, roasted vegetables, and tahini
- **Dinner**: Stuffed bell peppers with lentils and quinoa
- **Snacks**: Mixed nuts and dried fruit

### Day 5 (Friday - Workout Day)
- **Breakfast**: Whole-grain toast with avocado and hemp seeds
- **Lunch**: Spinach and mushroom wrap with hummus
- **Dinner**: Black bean and sweet potato chili
- **Snacks**: Greek yogurt with granola
- **Pre-Workout**: Small handful of mixed nuts
- **Post-Workout**: Smoothie with protein powder, banana, and spinach

### Days 6-7 (Weekend)
- **Saturday**: Focus on meal prep - batch cook grains and legumes
- **Sunday**: Relaxed cooking - try new vegetarian recipes

## Week 2 Meal Plan

### Day 8-14
Follow similar patterns with these additions:
- **New Breakfast Options**: Smoothie bowls, vegetarian breakfast burritos
- **New Lunch Options**: Falafel wraps, grain bowls with edamame
- **New Dinner Options**: Mushroom and spinach pasta, vegetarian stir-fries
- **Variety in Snacks**: Rice cakes with almond butter, energy balls

## Shopping Lists

### Week 1 Essentials
**Grains & Starches**: Quinoa, oats, brown rice, whole-grain bread, pasta
**Proteins**: Tofu, tempeh, legumes (chickpeas, lentils, black beans), Greek yogurt, cottage cheese
**Vegetables**: Spinach, bell peppers, mushrooms, tomatoes, carrots, avocado
**Fruits**: Bananas, berries, apples, kiwi, pineapple
**Pantry**: Nuts, seeds (chia, hemp, flax), nut butter, tahini, olive oil
**Beverages**: Almond milk, herbal teas

### Week 2 Additions
**New Proteins**: Edamame, protein powder
**New Vegetables**: Sweet potatoes, zucchini, broccoli
**New Pantry Items**: Curry paste, coconut milk, nutritional yeast

## Meal Prep Tips
1. **Batch Cook Grains**: Prepare quinoa and brown rice in large batches
2. **Pre-Portion Snacks**: Divide nuts and fruits into grab-and-go portions
3. **Prep Smoothie Packs**: Freeze smoothie ingredients in bags
4. **Make Dressings**: Prepare salad dressings and sauces in advance
5. **Wash and Chop**: Prep vegetables at beginning of week

## Hydration Guidelines
- **Daily Goal**: 8+ cups (64 oz) of water
- **Morning**: Start with glass of water upon waking
- **Pre-Workout**: 1-2 cups water 30 minutes before exercise
- **During Workout**: Sip water as needed
- **Post-Workout**: 2-3 cups to rehydrate
- **Evening**: Herbal teas (chamomile, peppermint) for relaxation

## Sleep-Supporting Foods
Include these in evening meals:
- **Magnesium-rich**: Spinach, pumpkin seeds, almonds
- **Tryptophan sources**: Chickpeas, oats, bananas
- **Complex carbs**: Quinoa, sweet potatoes, whole grains
- **Herbal teas**: Chamomile, passionflower, valerian root

## Foods to Limit for Better Sleep
- Caffeine after 2 PM
- Large meals within 3 hours of bedtime
- Excessive fluids 2 hours before bed
