# Alex's Complete 2-Week Wellness Program

## Program Overview
A comprehensive wellness program designed specifically for Alex, focusing on building consistent exercise habits, improving vegetarian nutrition, and managing stress/sleep - all while being mindful of mild anxiety and working with busy schedule constraints.

### Your Goals
- Build consistent exercise routine (3x/week, 30 minutes)
- Improve vegetarian diet for better energy
- Manage work stress effectively
- Optimize sleep quality for better rest and energy

## Week-by-Week Progression

### Week 1: Foundation Building
**Exercise Focus:**
- Learn proper form for all exercises
- Build habit of consistent timing
- Track how body responds
- Modify as needed for comfort

**Nutrition Focus:**
- Establish meal prep routine
- Try new vegetarian recipes
- Monitor energy levels throughout day
- Identify pre/post-workout preferences

**Stress/Sleep Focus:**
- Establish basic routines
- Practice breathing techniques
- Create optimal sleep environment
- Begin tracking stress and sleep quality

### Week 2: Optimization & Enhancement
**Exercise Progression:**
- Increase reps by 1-3 per exercise
- Extend plank holds slightly
- Reduce rest time between sets
- Add variety to prevent boredom

**Nutrition Enhancement:**
- Introduce new recipes and combinations
- Fine-tune pre/post-workout nutrition
- Optimize meal timing for energy
- Plan sustainable long-term approach

**Advanced Stress/Sleep Techniques:**
- Add Progressive Muscle Relaxation
- Extend meditation sessions to 10 minutes
- Implement more sophisticated work stress strategies
- Prepare for program sustainability

2. **Nutrition**: What meals provided sustained energy? What made you feel sluggish?

## Key Success Strategies

### For Building Exercise Consistency
1. **Prepare the Night Before**: Lay out clothes, prepare pre-workout snack
2. **Start Small**: Focus on showing up rather than perfect performance
3. **Track Progress**: Keep simple log of completed workouts
4. **Celebrate Wins**: Acknowledge each completed session

### For Nutrition Success
1. **Meal Prep**: Dedicate time weekly for preparation
2. **Flexible Structure**: Have backup meal options for busy days
3. **Listen to Body**: Notice how different foods affect energy and mood
4. **Stay Hydrated**: Consistent water intake throughout day

### For Stress/Sleep Improvement
1. **Consistency Over Perfection**: Same time daily, even if shorter
2. **Environment Optimization**: Create spaces that support relaxation
3. **Progressive Implementation**: Add one technique at a time
4. **Professional Support**: Consider therapy if anxiety persists

## Emergency Protocols

### If You Miss a Workout
- Do 10 minutes of movement (stretches, walk)
- Don't skip the next planned session
- Reflect on barriers and problem-solve

### If Stress Overwhelms
- Use 4-7-8 breathing technique immediately
- Step outside or change environment
- Use grounding technique (5-4-3-2-1)
- Reach out for support if needed

### If Sleep is Disrupted
- Don't panic - one bad night won't derail progress
- Return to wind-down routine following night
- Assess what may have caused disruption
- Adjust routine as needed
